## Scorecard

Score each dimension from 1 to 5.

- 1: weak, vague, unsupported, or strategically risky
- 3: plausible but needs refinement or validation
- 5: strong, specific, evidence-aligned, and execution-relevant

## Gate Rules

- PRD, ticket creation, growth, monetization, and build work remain blocked until this template has a verdict.
- Attack and Refine require human approval before advancing.
- Scores must not be changed silently; update the rationale and source artifacts when rescoring.
- Customer evidence must remain distinguishable from assumptions.
